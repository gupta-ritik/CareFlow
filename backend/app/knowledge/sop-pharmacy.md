## Turnaround
Discharge medicines should be prepared within 60 minutes of the discharge order. If preparation has not started within 60 minutes, the discharge coordinator may escalate the task to the duty pharmacist.

## Escalation
Escalate to the duty pharmacist with the task ID and order time. Set a follow-up check 30 minutes after escalation. Do not change the medicines or doses; only pharmacy staff and prescribers can do that.

## Medication reconciliation
The reconciliation form must be marked complete by a pharmacist before medicines are released. A form left in draft blocks discharge; send a reminder to the pharmacy queue.
