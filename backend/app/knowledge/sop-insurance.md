## Final authorization
For cashless patients, the final bill cannot be released until the payer or TPA sends final authorization. Typical turnaround is 90 minutes after submission.

## Payer queries
When the payer raises a query, check whether the requested document already exists in the record. If it exists, the insurance desk lead may approve attaching it to the claim. Never answer a query with a document that does not exist, and never mark a claim approved; only the payer can approve it.

## Portal outages
If the payer portal is unreachable, retry after 15 minutes. After repeated failures, the insurance desk lead decides whether to retry or contact the TPA by phone.
