## Acknowledgement
Final lab reports issued during the stay must be acknowledged by the ward before discharge so results are not lost. If reports are final but unacknowledged, ask the lab desk to request acknowledgement from the ward.

## Interpretation
CareFlow does not interpret results or judge their clinical significance. That stays with the treating team.
