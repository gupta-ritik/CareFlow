## Order of work
For cashless patients, billing starts the final bill only after insurance final authorization. For self-pay patients, billing starts immediately after clinical authorization.

## Delays
If the final bill is not ready 45 minutes after it could start, send a reminder to the billing counter. CareFlow never changes invoice amounts or records payments.
